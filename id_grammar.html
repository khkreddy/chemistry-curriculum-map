Qualification and UNBOUND

UNBOUND is legal: the taxonomy_bindings object is always present with fixed shape; members may be honestly empty. A dangling id (a written id that does not resolve) is not legal.

Joins that would have to guess from word overlap are banned. Empty members stay empty.
