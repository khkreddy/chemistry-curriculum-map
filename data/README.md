# Data

`chemistry_map_v14.json` is the PUBLIC map master: nodes, the 26 dependency edges with
use-contracts, the Big-Idea parentage layer, every syllabus statement with its seat and
depth and adjudication record, the retirement ledger, audit rulings, and the sign-off
chain. **It is a redacted projection**: misconception statements and mastery-facet
statements are internal to the owner and are withheld here — their ids, corruption types
and counts remain (see the `public_redaction` block). `chemistry_map_v13.json` is the
superseded predecessor under the same redaction.

`accm_canon.json` is the verbatim ACS ACCM extract used as the placement canon — all four
maps (general / physical / organic / inorganic), general canonical below the enduring
understandings.

`view_data5.json` is exactly what `index.html` draws. Generated by the V14 build, never
edited by hand; regenerate it from the master rather than editing.

`V14_BUILD_REPORT.md` records what changed, the adjudication counts, and the one recorded
deviation from the build playbook.

`pep/` holds the People's Education Press sidecars (teaching-sequence crosswalk material),
unchanged from V13. PEP text is machine-translated bridge English, unverified.
