# PEP ingestion — settled value verdict and form

Two independent assessments (`value_position_claude.md`, `value_position_kimi.md`, written
blind of each other), synthesised. The two minds converged on every load-bearing point;
kimi's form is stricter than claude's and the stricter form is adopted.

## The owner's question, answered jointly

**Does the map's node data (LO, Mx, mastery evidence) get better? NO — and it must not
be allowed to pretend to.** Both positions, independently:

- **Learning objectives: untouched.** PEP definitions are parallel statements; its
  Mastering/Understand tiers are gaokao-normed priority labels, not objectives.
- **Mx: untouched.** The file carries zero misconception models. The exercises' per-option
  analyses are *candidate raw material* for the CMS mx pipeline — entering solely through
  the existing witness-gated admission route as provenance-tagged proposals, never minted
  directly.
- **Mastery evidence: untouched.** The gating blocker (unit-level prerequisite graph,
  SETTLED_MODEL.md) is not removed by this file.
- **Topic coverage: redundant** — the same school chemistry the map already holds six ways.

**What is genuinely new** (joint ranking): (1) 270 attested exercises with per-option
rationales — the rarest metadata in item authoring; each is a generator family for ISO-GEN
(transform the surface, hold the discrimination structure), and the anchor set for
rehoming the 500 SUPERChem items (same national textbook ecosystem). (2) 309 structured
experiments — net-new practical-assessment templates (instruments, procedure, phenomena
as observable success criteria, verifies-links for remediation routing). (3) 1,344
prerequisite candidates — better than the co-location derivation the committee rejected,
because they encode deliberate sequencing by textbook authors; candidate evidence for the
gating task, one provenance vote each, never a bulk import. (4) The teaching sequence
itself — a third tradition's ordering for the atlas. Examples (1,444 lists): index-only;
kimi's argument accepted — no difficulty, no distractor logic, no built consumer.

## The adopted form (kimi's, stricter than claude's draft)

- **Map artifact carries**: `overlays.boards.PEP` — the 120-section crosswalk (section →
  map nodes, kimi-adjudicated, ~30 KB, same size class as other board overlays), marked
  `overlay_type: teaching_sequence` and pointing at the sidecar bundle; plus PEP tag
  counts in `node_projection` (the single namespaced attribute). **Nothing else**: no
  units minted, no content text, no PEP values in any map-native field, no new nodes, no
  new edges.
- **Sidecars** (`v10_build/`, referenced from the map):
  `pep_enrichment_raw.json` (exercises + experiments per section, MT English flagged
  bridge-text; examples indexed), `pep_prerequisite_candidates.json` (raw + node-level
  roll-up with intra-node edges discarded + corroboration table vs the 14 contracted
  edges; status CANDIDATE).
- **Teacher page**: no seventh lens band. A "taught through (PEP)" line in the pinned
  node panel from the crosswalk. Nothing else changes.
- **Guards** (kimi's three, binding): (1) edge-layer admission discipline unchanged —
  candidates live sidecar-only, any map edge without a use-contract is illegal, and no
  concept import to make edges resolve; (2) MT semantic risk — English is bridge text,
  `translation_quality: unverified_mt` stamped, known errata recorded (e.g. "bile alum"
  = copper(II) sulfate pentahydrate), original Chinese names retained where the file has
  them, full bilingual retention deferred until a zh source lands; (3) calibration —
  PEP difficulty/tiers are within-PEP relative orderings under a `pep.` namespace, never
  merged into band demands or mastery priors.

## Deviations between the positions, ruled

- Claude proposed capped in-map exemplars; kimi ruled content out of the map entirely.
  **Kimi's rule adopted** — the owner's "enrich the node with the example" is served by
  the sidecar + panel line without bloating the artifact.
- Claude proposed structural ingestion of example lists; kimi ruled index-only.
  **Kimi's rule adopted.**
- Kimi suggested human sign-off per crosswalk row. The map's standing evidence standard
  is "model-adjudicated, NOT owner-verified" for every overlay; the crosswalk ships at
  that standard, disclosed as always. Owner review remains open.
